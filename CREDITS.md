# Credits

## Images

No stock photography is used. All illustrations (particle scenes, stage stills, product silhouettes, heaps, map drawings, the placeholder logo and Open Graph images) are generated in code for this site.

All fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- **Fraunces** by Undercase Type (Phaedra Charles, Flavia Zimbardi): display type. Served through `next/font/google`; WOFF copies in `src/og/fonts` render Open Graph images.
- **Schibsted Grotesk** by Schibsted and Bakken & Bæck: text and interface. Served through `next/font/google`.
- **IBM Plex Mono** by IBM: labels and specification sheets. Served through `next/font/google`; a WOFF copy is in `src/og/fonts`.
